# P7 human mobile QA and balance checks

**Target:** 390×844 iPhone 13 viewport, also short/narrow screens and reduced-motion mode.

- Poker legibility: suits/ranks identifiable under effects; preflop action order; pot/call/raise/all-in amounts visible; side pots and chip returns explicitly animated and logged.
- Strategic decisions: player knows what is at stake, no chips as HP; pot-limit Briar tell, public hole-card tell, spell cost, valid target selection, save after every action.
- Life/contract: zero-chip freeze, named creditor, refusal and counteroffer, one-roll replay, funded rescue, mandatory no erotic scenes; no double rescue on reload.
- World route: no repeat table entry; Writ fee at most once, Pass at most once, Cinder/Underworld ingress at most once, endings archive with exact balances.
- Magic: 8 spell cards/3 drawn/1 cast max, Aether cap 3, resonance instance 1:1 and branch replacement, first rare relic consumption clearly explained.
- Human playtest: recruit 5–8 naïve players; track first legal action time, misunderstanding of chip totals, skill understanding, unexpected death, expressed enjoyment after 15 and 45 min; log sessions but do not change difficulty from tiny sample without interpreting context.
- Automated: ≥2,000 runs per policy per configuration across multiple independent seeds, report phase conditional outcomes, multiway and all-in regression, no losses of invariants.
