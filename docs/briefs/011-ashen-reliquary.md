# Report #011: Ashen Reliquary (design system v3)

*2026-10-27. The design system was rebuilt from the owner's reference set (the board, two mock-ups, the back, four emblems and four portraits). The full system is described in `docs/design-system-v3.md`.*

The before/after pairs in `docs/briefs/011/` use the same save state on both sides. "Before" is v1.2 (commit `03919fd`). All captures are automated browser emulation, not real phones.

## What changed

- **Palette.** Brass, vellum and soot became oxidized copper, bone ivory and coal. Smoked plum is used for magic, bruised vermilion for Lifechips, seals and the decisive action, and muted celadon for check and call.
- **Type.** Cinzel Decorative for titles, Cinzel for labels, Spectral for all text. Alegreya Sans, Cormorant Garamond and Inter were removed.
- **Controls.** Every button is a rectangular framed plate: copper corner brackets, an inner rule and brushed grain. The fill names the action:
  - Fold: coal;
  - Check and call: celadon;
  - Raise: copper;
  - The decisive action: wax red;
  - A Cinder raise: ember glow.
- **Title.**
  - The wordmark sits over an eclipse ring with the Ledger's rule through it.
  - The Ledger Adjudicator is the hero portrait.
  - Menu plates have an icon well, a title, one line of description and a chevron.
  - An "At the tables" row shows Lady Vesper, the Sightless Oracle and Sir Corvin.
- **Table.** Generated by us, with no external art:
  - a coal-stone slab in an oxidized copper rim, with a carved ritual circle under the pot and the board;
  - the pot sits in a copper cartouche;
  - cards are bone stock with stains, a copper frame and vermilion suits.
- **Spells.** Each slip carries its discipline's painted emblem in the corner. The spell list shows the emblem at 44 px and evolution choices at 34 px. The 14 px line sigils stay where the paintings would turn to mud.
- **Last Claim.** The P3 green-and-gold title card is toned to coal and copper. The contract is unchanged.
- **App icon.** Recoloured to copper to match.

1. **Portraits.** The cut-outs had semi-transparent dark halos with red and green colour fringes. The matte was tightened, the fringes recoloured, and each portrait trimmed to its painted disc on the coal ground at 512 px. Nothing was repainted.
2. **Reliquary back.** The card was cropped from its surround. The game's gate requires a back to read the same both ways up, so the upper half was rotated into the lower half; the central eclipse therefore shows two crescents. The back was given the standard silhouette and a 2 px copper edge keyline: without it the edge measures 2.2:1 against the new table, below WCAG's 3:1.

**Status.** All of the above passes the machine gates and is **delivered**. Making them **integrated** needs a named reviewer, for example:

```sh
python3 -I scripts/check-art.py --apply --approve vesper --reviewer NAME
```

Provenance notes say the files were supplied by the owner and that the generation method was not stated.

## Assets from the internet

- **Fonts.** Cinzel Decorative and Spectral were added from npm (`@fontsource`), both under the SIL Open Font License.
- **Textures.** CC0 texture libraries (ambientCG, Poly Haven) and Wikimedia are blocked by this environment's network policy; only npm, GitHub and Google Fonts are reachable. Every texture is therefore generated: SVG turbulence for paper, metal and stone, and the table and card ageing in `scripts/build-assets.mjs`. Nothing unlicensed is in the build.
