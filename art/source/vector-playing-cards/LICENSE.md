# Vector Playing Cards — public domain

Court-card artwork (J, Q, K) and the ornate Ace of Spades used as source for our
generated card faces come from **Vector Playing Cards** by Byron Knoll
(<http://www.byronknoll.com/>), released into the public domain
(optionally WTFPL where public domain is not recognized).

Source: <https://github.com/notpeter/Vector-Playing-Cards>, mirroring
<https://code.google.com/archive/p/vector-playing-cards/>. See CONTRIBUTORS.md.

`scripts/build-assets.mjs` recolors them to the art-bible palette; no other changes.
